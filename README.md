## Will it work in your pool?

**In short:** the A013 and A012 floor cleaners suit pool floors of about 100 m², up to 2 m deep, with no steps and no slopes over 23°. The B011, B012 and B016 solar skimmers suit outdoor pools of 200, 180 and 150 m² that get regular sun. Floating cables, strong pump inlets and infinity edges can stop a skimmer, so check them first.

Before you put your brand on a model, trial a sample in pools like your customers'.

### Floor cleaners: A013 and A012

What they do: collect leaves, sand, small stones and other everyday debris from the pool floor. They are basic floor cleaners: everyday floor cleaning at a controlled purchase cost.

| Check | Fits | Doesn't fit, or needs a trial |
|---|---|---|
| Pool area | About 100 m² (standard configuration) | Larger pools; also check the shape, not just the area |
| Depth and water | Up to 2 m; water 5–35 °C | Deeper pools or water outside the range |
| Steps | – | Steps are unsuitable |
| Slopes | Flat floors | Slopes over 23° are unsuitable; slopes at or below 23° still need a trial in the actual pool |
| Floor shape | Regular floors | Potholes and tight corners may trap the robot |
| Walls | – | No wall climbing; the floor only |

How they behave:

- Motor thrust is adjusted by travel speed: less on the flat, more on slopes and when turning.
- When the filter clogs and speed keeps dropping, the robot treats the basket as full and parks at the wall. Below 10% battery it also parks at the wall. Either way it is lifted out by hand.
- Escape, wall-parking and reverse-on-contact are basic travel controls, not obstacle avoidance, mapping or wall climbing.
- Runtime is 120–180 minutes from a full charge; charging takes 2.5–3 hours.

Care that affects returns: use the original authorized charger, keep the charging port dry, fit the filter before starting, drain for 10–15 seconds after lifting the robot out, and charge to 70% or more every 3 months in storage.

Official pages: [A013](https://www.mydeepcleaner.com/products/a013), [A012](https://www.mydeepcleaner.com/products/a012), [choosing an A Series floor cleaner](https://www.mydeepcleaner.com/news/choosing-a-series-pool-floor-cleaners), [what a floor-only robot can and can't do](https://www.mydeepcleaner.com/news/floor-only-pool-robot-expectations).

### Solar skimmers: B011, B012 and B016

What they do: collect leaves, pollen, dust and other floating debris from the surface of outdoor pools, before it sinks.

| Model | Pool area | Basket | Solar panel | Size |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [B011](https://www.mydeepcleaner.com/products/b011) | 200 m² | 4.5 L | 15.8 W | 540×460×180 mm |
| [B012](https://www.mydeepcleaner.com/products/b012) | 180 m² | 4 L | 9.4 W | 520×460×190 mm |
| [B016](https://www.mydeepcleaner.com/products/b016) | 150 m² | 4 L | 12 W | 415×335×170 mm |

| Check | Fits | Doesn't fit, or needs a trial |
|---|---|---|
| Sunlight | Outdoor pools with regular sun | Indoor pools; long runs of cloudy or rainy days drain the battery, and it stops until recharged with the adapter |
| Floating cables | – | Cables on the surface can tangle it |
| Pump inlet | Normal circulation | A strong pump inlet can hold it in place |
| Steps and large debris | – | May need manual handling |
| Pool edge | Pools with a wall above the water | Wall detection is ultrasonic; on infinity or edge-flush pools the edge may not be detected |
| Water temperature | 10–35 °C | Outside the range |

Reverse and extra-thrust escape moves help, but they don't guarantee release from cables, strong inlets or steps. Coverage comes from running for long periods, not from route planning.

Power and control:

- Sunlight powers the motors first; any surplus charges the battery. With sun available, it starts automatically at 6.9 V.
- From a full battery with no solar input, every B Series model runs about **25 hours in low-speed mode**. Running all day every day depends on consecutive sunny days.
- The adapter charges it in about 3 hours (8.4 V, 1.8 A).
- Over Bluetooth, at about 15 m, the app shows solar charging status and battery level, switches speed and steers. There is no remote control over the internet.

Care: keep the solar panel, sensor, impeller, roller brush and basket clean. The flexible solar panel resists corrosion but not impact; a knock can break it. The basket drains on four sides and rinses under a tap. Charge it fully once if stored for more than 3 months. The motors are DiBiao Tech's own; a 3,000-hour aging test in a pool ended with the skimmer running normally, and each skimmer opens with three screws for repair.
